From product link to parcel

  1. Check the listing and size

    Open the HipobuyFind product page and choose LitBuy from the Buy Link menu if available. Confirm the item, color, size and current price in LitBuy. For clothing, compare the seller’s flat measurements with a garment you own.

  2. Review warehouse QC photos

    When warehouse photos are available, compare the size label, color, stitching and any visible damage with the listing. If a measurement matters, ask whether a photo with a measuring tape is available and check the return window.

  3. Estimate the delivered cost

    Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges. Check whether the shipping quote uses actual or volumetric weight, especially for boxed shoes and bulky jackets.

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Can I download the spreadsheet?

Tick the listings you want to compare and choose “Download shortlist CSV.” The file includes product names, item IDs, approximate USD prices and links, plus blank columns for size, QC status and shipping estimates.

Where do the product listings come from?

The product cards point to individual HipobuyFind listings. This independent guide uses a catalog snapshot. Check the linked listing for changes in price, stock and buying options.

What if the LitBuy link is missing or expired?

Check the original marketplace listing from the product page. If it is active, try its original link in LitBuy and confirm that the seller, photos and variants match before ordering. If the source has expired, look for another listing with a current size chart and seller details.
